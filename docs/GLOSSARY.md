# Glossary of AI Terms

The purpose of this document is to explain the less common terms of the AI technology and techniques we use in this project.

## Embeddings

Embeddings are mathematical representations of data—like text, images, or audio—translated into long sequences of numbers (vectors).

## Inference

In AI, inference refers to the process where an existing trained AI model sees new data for the first time and analyses it to make decisions, predictions, or generate new content.

## Lemma / Lemmatization

In linguistics and AI, a lemma is the base, dictionary form of a word. Lemmatization is the process of grouping together different inflected forms of a word so they can be analyzed as a single root meaning. Lemmatization is a vital Natural Language Processing (NLP) technique.

## Model Runner

A model runner is software built to run AI models, popular model runners include Ollama, LM Studio.

## OOV, Out Of Vocabulary

When an input word doesn't exist within the AI's vector space.

## Quantization

Quantization in AI is the process of reducing the precision of a model’s numerical values—like weights and activations—from high-precision formats (such as 32-bit floating-point) to lower-precision formats (such as 8-bit or 4-bit integers). This drastically reduces memory usage and speeds up processing, allowing large AI models to run on cheaper or mobile hardware.

## Synset

A Synset (short for "synonym set") is a specific group of interchangeable synonyms that represent a single, distinct concept. It is the fundamental building block of lexical databases and artificial intelligence tools like WordNet.

In a standard thesaurus, words are grouped solely by meaning. Synsets do much more, by differentiating word senses and linking them through semantic relationships.
Synsets handle disambiguation of words which have many meanings, and group variations of words which have the same meaning.

## Semantic Triple

In AI, a semantic triple (or RDF triple) is the fundamental data structure used to represent knowledge as distinct facts. It breaks information down into three parts in a simple Subject → Predicate → Object format to make data machine-readable

## Token

In AI systems, a token is a unit of text used as input to a model. Tokens are typically sub-word units rather than whole words — for example, "embeddings" might be split into "embed" and "dings". A token can be a word, part of a word, or a single character.

## Vectors

A vector is a mathematical representation of a position within a space. You may be familiar with vectors in the real world as three dimensional coordinates, x, y and z. In AI systems the number of coordinates in a vector is, in theory, unlimited, but commonly limited to hundreds or thousands of dimensions such as 256, 768 or 1536 dimensions.

## Vector space

The number of dimensions available for storing vectors.

## Word2Vec

Word2Vec is a natural language processing technique that converts words into dense numerical vectors. By analyzing word context, it maps words with similar meanings to closely located points in a multidimensional space.

## WordNet

A popular open-source lexical database widely used in NLP tasks such as semantic similarity, word sense disambiguation, and text classification.
